# Rulebook Lens - 2 minute company demo

**0:00-0:15 - Problem**
“University policy is often split across handbooks, circulars, spreadsheets, and exception notices. Rulebook Lens gives students and staff answers they can verify, not chatbot guesses.”

**0:15-0:40 - Citation-first answer**
Ask: “Can I sit an exam with 68% attendance?” Show the conflict result and its three citation cards. Explain that 75% is the ordinary rule while certified medical absence permits 65%; the system exposes both instead of choosing silently.

**0:40-1:00 - Safe abstention**
Ask: “Can I bring my pet parrot to lectures?” Show the Not covered outcome. Explain that it declines to invent rules when the corpus is silent.

**1:00-1:20 - Human-in-the-loop**
Run the attendance question again. Click “Route for review.” Scroll to the Policy Operations review queue and show the new open case, evidence count, then mark it resolved.

**1:20-1:42 - Governance evidence**
Scroll to Evidence Lab. Call out 6,000+ indexed words, 59 passages, three known conflict tests, and 25 abstention tests. Click a known conflict test to demonstrate repeatable QA.

**1:42-2:00 - Deployment and close**
Open GitHub and show README, Dockerfile, tests, and the API documentation at `/docs`. Close with: “Rulebook Lens is a transparent policy-assistance layer: evidence first, human escalation for ambiguity, and deployment-ready from day one.”
